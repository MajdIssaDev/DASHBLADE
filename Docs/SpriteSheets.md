# DASHBLADE sprite sheets

These sprites are for the F08 visual pass. F01’s Play Mode checklist still uses a white circle, red nose, grey walls, and a brown door marker.

| Sheet | Sprites |
| --- | --- |
| `dashblade-actors.png` | Masked swordsman, Charger, Turret |
| `dashblade-arena.png` | Repeatable slate floor and stone wall, closed entrance door, amber lantern |
| `dashblade-arena-props.png` | Intact and broken barrels, storage crate, supply sacks, iron chain, rubble, floor drain, moss, stone pillar stump |
| `dashblade-combat-hud.png` | Dash trail, slash arc, impact, Charger lane warning, Turret ring warning and projectile, full and empty hearts, dash ready and recharging |

The sheets live in `Assets/_Game/Art/SpriteSheets`. Their Unity import settings name each slice and set 128 pixels per unit. The swordsman and both machines face right (+X) at zero rotation. The swordsman's pivot sits on his body. The dash trail's pivot sits at its leading edge, and the slash arc's pivot sits at its origin.

The floor slice covers 2 × 2 world units, the wall covers 1 × 1, and the door covers 1 × 2. In the F01 test footprint, 12 × 6 floor tiles cover the 24 × 12 interior. The door fits on the west wall at y = 0. Set collision and warning timing in gameplay when integrating the sheets. Place the amber lanterns by the walls so the orange lane and ring remain easy to read on the arena floor.

The prop slices have centered pivots. Barrels, crates, sacks and the pillar stump can dress the arena perimeter. The chain, rubble, drain and moss can add detail to the slate floor.

## Image sources

The built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool generated the actor and arena source sheets, the floor texture, and the arena props for this project. The selected sprites were cropped and packed into transparent PNGs. The stone textures were adjusted to match their opposing edges. The combat effects, warnings, and HUD glyphs were drawn and rasterized locally.
